## Synthesis for the manuscript

- AQI forecasting is established; direct India-specific work includes
  statistical and machine-learning approaches [1]–[5].
- Exact lead time, target construction, and future availability are often not
  ascertainable from abstracts. Do not compare reported headline scores until
  full methods, splits, and targets have been checked.
- Time-aware evaluation is a general methodological concern [7]–[10].
  The present chronological protocol is a defensible design choice, not a
  novelty claim.
- The present data's repeated city identities do not create an unseen-city
  test. Any future city-transfer claim requires a distinct city-holdout
  design with no target-city tuning.
- The project's limited candidate contribution is the audited comparison
  of exact-calendar baselines with native coverage and shared-case errors
  under two AQI policies. Novelty remains unestablished.
